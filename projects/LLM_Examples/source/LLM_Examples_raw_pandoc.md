# Writing Assistance

The LLMs are built for writing. There are a lot of aspects to writing. The examples here look into how this tool can be beneficial.

## Dictation

Creating a photo-essay is a practice we often follow during our travels. We do this for most of our big trips as a way to document what we\'ve seen and done for future reference. It also allows us to reflect on specific aspects of our trip in more detail (e.g. by closely examining the photos) and learn things that will improve our future travels. However, it can be a lot of work to record and document each day\'s activities, particularly with regards to the writing, typing, and editing process. This can sometimes lead to delays in completing our essays, which can be frustrating and lead to feelings of guilt over unfinished projects.

This Dictation Test is an attempt to streamline the process of creating photo-essays by focusing on text entry and editing. The goal is to make it possible to produce essays in near real-time.

The following experiment will test this approach. In the following text, comments and ChatGPT requests are given in bold, while ChatGPT responses are in normal font.

**The following paragraph was dictated into a Samsung Tab S8+ tablet using the microphone in Google Docs. This is a made-up story created only for testing.**

**As you can see, there are none of the usual sentence markers (e.g., commas, periods and caps).**

We went from downtown Tokyo to Akihabara there we were looking for a camera store we needed to buy some memory for the Sony camera this is very expensive in the US but hopefully the yen to dollar conversion rate will make it less expensive in Japan once we had gotten that we were very hungry what\'s the best thing to do in Tokyo of course it\'s to seek out ramen so that was our next goal find a ramen shop we\'re not too particular as we\'re not ramen experts but we wanted something good or at least something typical of Japan so we walked for quite a few blocks looking at the advertisements in the windows looking at the plastic models on display in front of each restaurant it was after 20 minutes or so that we were getting pretty hungry and we decided that it was time to make a decision

**The next thing to do was to clean up the text. ChatGPT was opened, "Please fix the following paragraph." was typed in and the Google Doc text was pasted following the request. Here is the result.**

> We went from downtown Tokyo to Akihabara, where we were looking for a camera store to buy some memory for our Sony camera. This can be very expensive in the US, but we hoped the yen to dollar conversion rate would make it less expensive in Japan. Once we had gotten what we needed, we were very hungry and decided to seek out some ramen. We\'re not ramen experts, but we wanted something good or at least something typical of Japan. We walked for quite a few blocks, looking at the advertisements in the windows and the plastic models on display in front of each restaurant. After about 20 minutes, we were getting pretty hungry and decided it was time to make a decision.

You can see that the changes were a bit more than an addition of punctuation. It smoothed out the story a bit, too. **What followed were a few additional prompts to ChatGPS, mostly asking for changes in the tense of the story.** These worked fine.

**The last two steps asked for a conversion into past perfect tense, then a request to "improve the latest version."** The following text was the result.

> We had just arrived in Akihabara, a neighborhood known for its electronics stores, and were on the hunt for a camera store to buy some memory for our Sony camera. We knew that this type of equipment could be very expensive in the United States, but we hoped that the favorable exchange rate between the yen and the dollar would make it more affordable in Japan. After we had found what we needed, we were famished and set our sights on finding a tasty bowl of ramen. Although we weren\'t experts on the subject, we wanted to try something authentic or at least representative of Japan. We walked for several blocks, taking in the sights and sounds of the bustling street and examining the plastic food displays and advertisements in the windows of various restaurants. It wasn\'t long before our stomachs began to growl, and we decided it was time to make a decision and choose a place to eat.

This appears to be a remarkable way to gain efficiency when producing documents of this genre. Recall that this document was dictated on a tablet, there were a few simple cut-and-paste operations, and the few commands were given to ChatGPT.

Here are a few observations:

- The text needs some editing to fix the tense. The overall structure might be improved by breaking the narrative into paragraphs.

- The story has been embellished. Is this a correct reflection of the situation?

- The results seem to have more "energy" as some visceral emotions (e.g., "stomachs began to growl") have replaced fairly bland statements (e.g., "getting pretty hungry").

This result shows there is considerable promise using a combination of dictation and AI-based editing as a way to produce the text for photo-essays in "near real time."

## Association Index

Some years ago, Marsh Fisher developed an "Association Index." He spent millions of dollars on this. It involved hiring professionals in many fields to list terms and phrases that are relevant in a particular domain. His product never took off as a commercial success even though it has great utility.

The following tests whether ChatGPT can do the same sort of work as Fisher's Association Index.

**What are words and phrases associated with the color orange?**

> Here are some words and phrases that are commonly associated with the color orange:

- Citrus

- Warmth

- Energy

- Vibrancy

- Enthusiasm

- Creativity

- Fun

- Playful

- Excitement

- Halloween (as a complementary color to black)

- Harvest

- Autumn (as a color associated with the changing leaves)

- Sunsets

- Citrus fruits (e.g., oranges, tangerines, etc.)

- Traffic cones (as a safety symbol)

- Tiger (as a reference to the animal\'s stripes)

- Basketball (as the color of the ball)

- Fire (as a symbol of warmth and heat)

- Spice (as a color associated with many spices, such as paprika, turmeric, etc.)

> These are just a few examples, and the associations with the color orange can vary depending on cultural, historical, and personal contexts.

Several other words were used as prompts. All of the tests gave acceptable results. The conclusion is that this is a pretty good substitute for the Fisher software (which is no longer available).

The algorithm starts by selecting K initial centroids (points that represent the center of the clusters), and then iteratively assigns each data point to the nearest centroid and updates the centroid based on the mean value of the points in that cluster. This process continues until the centroids no longer move significantly, at which point the algorithm converges.
>
> The output of the K-means analysis is a set of K clusters, where each data point is assigned to one of the clusters based on its similarity to the centroid. >
> K-means is a useful technique for discovering underlying patterns or structure in data, identifying outliers, and reducing the dimensionality of data by grouping similar data points together. It is widely used in fields such as data science, pattern recognition, and image processing.
