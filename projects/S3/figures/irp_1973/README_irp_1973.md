# IRP 1973 weekly rain, Mauna Loa transect (transcribed 2026-11-27)

Source: Bridges, K. W. and G. V. Carey (1974). *Climate data for the IBP sites on Mauna Loa, Hawaii.*
Technical Report No. 38, Island Ecosystems IRP, U.S. International Biological Program, May 1974.
Scan: Kim's marked-up copy (IBP_TR38.pdf, 109 pages). Tables "Weekly rain record in mm", pdf pages 91-98.

Files
- am_std_1973.csv: weekly rain (mm) from the single standard gauge ("AM STD") at each site, 52 weeks.
  Dates are YYMMDD (the week's collection date). K54 week 730312 = NA (standard gauge read 0.0 while
  the other 15 gauges averaged about 93 mm: a gauge fault).
- overall_mean_1973.csv: the report's "overall mean" for the two 16-gauge sites (K54, M54).

Gauges: K54 and M54 had 16 gauges (11 Tru-Check, 1 standard, 4 metric); M42 and M66 had the standard gauge only.

Checks
- M42 standard-gauge total 1,390.9 mm and M66 1,657.0 mm match the report's cumulative-rain figures (pp. 71, 73).
- K54 overall-mean total 1,587.9 mm matches the report's figure (p. 70, about 1,590); M54 925.3 mm vs about 910 (p. 72).
- Overflow: in the weeks of 731119 and 731126 every Tru-Check gauge reads 152.4 mm (6 in) and every metric gauge
  231.0 mm -- their capacities. Only the standard gauge recorded the full storm (e.g., M54 694.7 mm in one week).
  The overall mean therefore understates those weeks; standard-gauge totals: K54 2,176.1, M54 1,695.9 mm
  (vs overall-mean 1,587.9 and 925.3).

Transcription: Claude, by OCR plus reading each page image; every value of the standard-gauge and overall columns
read from the page; OCR self-checks (overall = weighted mean of gauge types) used to flag misreads.
