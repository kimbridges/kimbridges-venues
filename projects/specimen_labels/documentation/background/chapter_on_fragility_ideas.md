## Threads
1. **Name the worry straight.** Leans on QR codes (which fail), cards drop/soak, phones die; "light is not a
   word that inspires trust in a record meant to last centuries."
2. **The code redundancy.** A card prints value + field name + code — "the same fact three ways"; a failed
   scan costs a minute, not a specimen.
3. **Cheap stacked redundancies.** Chimp the shot; a second photographer / second phone; carry two card sets.
   "cheap insurance is exactly the kind worth buying."
4. **★ The floor (graceful degradation).** Identity = number card + photograph (who/where/when in one frame);
   observation cards are enrichment on top. In a downpour, number card + one shot, move on. "You are never
   left with nothing."
5. **★ The friction tie (Kim's key insight).** Attend to little → watch it well; the old way over-taxed finite
   attention. "Fragility and friction turn out to be the same thing seen twice, and lightening the one
   strengthens against the other."
6. **★ Extensibility = the other half of not-fragile (Kim's new idea).** Special needs (e.g., a full roll call
   of neighboring species) are met by **photographing several scenes, each a group of species cards** — the
   same act, no new friction. "A method that grows by repeating its single move... is a sturdy method, not a
   brittle one." (Flexibility reframed as a *kind* of robustness.)
7. **Close.** "light is not the same as weak... fails softly, doubles for pennies, bends to what a collector
   needs, stands on a floor that always holds." → hands to the Field Log finale.
