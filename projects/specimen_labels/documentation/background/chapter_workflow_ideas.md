## The field procedure (Kim's exact sequence, 2026-07-03) — SINGLE identity card
1. Collect the specimen; **assess the plant and its environment** (observe while fresh).
2. Lay out the cards, **identity card first**, then **rotate each observation deck** to the card that fits.
3. **One photograph** of the whole layout, **close to the plant** (the photo's location is what the camera's
   GPS records — the coordinate is the point).
4. **Chimp the shot** (all cards present + in focus); **optional second photographer** (two copies, two
   phones). *(Steps here; the reasoning is Ch. 11's.)*
5. **Then** tie the identity card to the specimen (string through the punched hole, can't work loose).
6. **Bag last** — drop the tagged specimen into the collection bag.
The identity card is a **single card**: photographed in the layout, then tied on as the specimen's tag. It is
the link between specimen and data, in the photo and on the plant.

## Before-the-field details (Kim)
Print → **separate the cards, punch a hole in one end** (the hole ties an ID card to a specimen OR spindles a
deck onto its screw post). Decks are **not fixed for years** — cards are added/retired as focus/region shifts;
the numbers are reprinted every trip.

## At-the-desk / outputs
Folder of photos → batch → one record each. Outputs: **Darwin Core file** (herbarium prints its own on
archival paper), **provisional labels** (fallback), and the records as the **"starting material for the field
log"** (NOT a finished log — Kim: don't imply the log is complete; the finale builds it).
