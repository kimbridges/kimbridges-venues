## Threads
1. **The handoff.** "In the last chapter the integrator was you. Now it's the phone." One act (a photograph)
   replaces the many-instrument kit.
2. **The photograph is the record, and it comes stamped.** Camera makes the record passively; GPS + clock
   write place and time into the file. "The two things the old notebook got wrong most often... are now the
   two things you can't get wrong. They arrive for free, and they arrive correct."
3. **The fixed-by variables are derived, not measured.** The coordinate retires the altimeter/clinometer/
   compass/map; elevation, slope, aspect, place, rock are looked up later from open data ("free and without
   permission, so the answer comes out the same whoever asks" — light equity seed, kept to one sentence; Ch.
   9 develops it; Ch. 6 opens the derive engine). Kept deliberately light here.
4. **The judged-from variables need recording, and writing is the friction.** Narrows to: "how do you record
   a choice without writing it? You photograph it. Not the plant, the choice itself." (Kim caught that a bare
   "You photograph it." reads as "photograph the plant"; the fix names the choice. Now literally true given
   Thread 6.)
5. **The QR reframe (the reveal).** A QR code is not "a link you scan"; it's "a way of printing a short piece
   of text so a camera can read it back." Ours holds plain words ("Life Form: Tree"). Vindication: a naive
   scan shows readable text, not a broken link. **FIGURE 1:** a single card, value + QR, the
   scan-returns-plain-text demo.
6. **★ The photograph is of the CARDS, not the specimen (Kim, 2026-07-02) — the key simplification.** The
   plant is never in the frame. It's already **bagged**; one card carries its **collection number**, which is
   the **stand-in** for the specimen (the Ch. 2 ID-tag thread). Lay the cards out flat and well lit, take one
   dry shot. Removes framing friction ("nothing wet, drippy, sticky, or pokey"), improves decode reliability
   (flat + lit codes read cleanly), and matches what we actually tested (cards on a table, no plant).
   **FIGURE 2:** a set of cards laid out for one shot, the number card among them. **This refined the project
   spine one-liner** — corrected in `design_notes.md`, `proj_specimen_labels.md`, `project_index.md`.
7. **The integrator, one act.** Name the set: camera (making the record), GPS (georeferencing), cards (the
   writing), open data (the instrument kit) — each retires one friction, and nothing is reconciled later
   because nothing was split. "Now it's the phone."
8. **Forward close.** "One act, one record... What that single record becomes... is the next thing to see.
   The picture is taken. Now we read it." (→ Ch. 4.)
