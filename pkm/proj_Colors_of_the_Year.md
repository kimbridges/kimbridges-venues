## Type
Quarto multi-chapter document

## Objective
A data-driven document examining the published "Colors of the Year"
(Pantone and related) — their color-space distribution, evolution
across years, volatility, and the moods or themes they project. The
document combines analysis with reflection: data chapters
characterize the sequence quantitatively, narrative chapters
interpret it. Slated to be the first real exercise of the audio
workflow (see proj_audio.md).
