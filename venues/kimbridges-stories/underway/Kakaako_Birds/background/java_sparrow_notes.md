# Java Sparrow — working notes

Background and follow-up for `Stories/java_sparrow.md`. Same rules: nothing is
published until written into the story with a `[V:]` tag.

## Sources (literature-confirmed claims)
- **Introduced to O‘ahu, 1960s (cage trade).** First documented O‘ahu sighting
  1964 (19th-c. attempts failed); established from released/escaped cage birds;
  spread to other main islands 1982–86; breeding populations esp. on O‘ahu.
  Native to Java and Bali (+ small nearby islands). Names: Java Rice Bird, Java
  Finch, "temple bird"; *oryzivora* = "rice-eater." (Bishop Museum RLP monograph
  JASP.pdf; Hawai‘i Birding Trails; birdsofhawaii.info; Wikipedia.)
- **Distinctive ID.** Pale-gray body, black cap + tail, white cheek patch, red
  eye-ring, heavy pink-red conical bill, pink legs. "Immediately distinctive."
  (Cornell All About Birds; Wikipedia; BotW.)
- **Popular cage bird, for beauty.** Among the most popular cage birds
  worldwide; kept for looks + calm nature; widely introduced. (Wikipedia; Bali
  Bird Park; earthlife.)
- **Endangered at home (the irony).** In native range, disastrous declines from
  cage-trade trapping; IUCN **Endangered**, <10,000 mature wild individuals,
  still declining; up to ~38% of adults trapped/year in central & eastern Java
  (2004–05); gregarious flocking makes it easy for trappers. Once abundant in
  Bali rice fields, now hard to find. (BirdLife/IUCN; FNPF; Bali Bird Park.)

## Kim-observed anchors / frame [V: Kim]

## Thematic notes
- **Cage-trade cluster (vector 4), THIRD seed-eater of it** with the saffron
  finch + waxbill (and the bulbul as the 4th cage-trade bird overall). In the
  reorder, sits near the cage-trade newcomers; also rhymes with the red-crested
  cardinal ("brought for beauty" / the Hui Manu aesthetic impulse).
- **Mirror of the koloa (deliberate echo).** Koloa = endemic vanishing AT home
  (Hawai‘i) by hybridization; Java sparrow = vanishing at ITS home (Java) by
  trapping while thriving here as an exile. Two conservation "fractures" facing
  opposite directions; the draft draws the parallel explicitly. Strengthens the
  book's "fractures / it's us" spine and gives an introduced bird real
  conservation weight.
- **Beauty as double-edge.** The trait that spread it worldwide is the trait
  endangering it at home — a clean statement of the book's recurring idea that
  the bird–human relationship cuts more than one way.
